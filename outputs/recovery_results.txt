Networkwalks Week 3 (B084) - Password recovery of 3 locked PDFs
Lab files: My Locked PDF1.pdf, My Locked PDF2.pdf, My Locked PDF3.pdf
All test files provided by Networkwalks for the lab; recovered for learning only.

Method A - John the Ripper format hash, cracked offline (hashcat, mode 10500):
  pdf2john "My Locked PDF1.pdf" > pdf_hashes.txt
  hashcat -m 10500 -a 0 pdf_hashes.txt rockyou.txt
  Result: 3/3 recovered.

Recovered passwords:
  My Locked PDF1.pdf  ->  password1
  My Locked PDF2.pdf  ->  password1
  My Locked PDF3.pdf  ->  1qaz2wsx

Note: the hash the Networkwalks Hash Calculator produces is pdf2john / hashcat compatible,
so the identical John the Ripper command recovers the same passwords:
  john --wordlist=/usr/share/wordlists/rockyou.txt pdf_hashes.txt
